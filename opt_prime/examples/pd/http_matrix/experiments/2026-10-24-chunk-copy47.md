## Question

Can producer compute advance while host-staged KV copies are pending, preserving exact KV and token output, prefix offsets, and safe recovery after a transfer error?

## Setup

Opt-in TorchPrefillEngine copy_overlap and serving flag --pd-prefill-copy-overlap. A single copy worker uses dedicated source/destination streams and at most two pending gathered spans. Destination KV remains request-owned and is returned only after all copies finish. The prefill_s counter includes queue backpressure; transfer_s measures only the unhidden tail, not total DMA time. GPU-to-GPU only; RNGD/ATOM transports are unchanged. Empty spans exposed an existing PagedKV slot-index dtype bug, fixed by explicitly using long indices.
