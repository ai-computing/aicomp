The independent auditor checks actual arrays/text, lengths, condition identities, resource metadata and timing arithmetic. Whole/stream references match4/4 per model. All children and the coordinator exit0. Fresh queries confirm no GPU compute processes and no holders on the selected local RNGD device files; the separate remote ATOM sweep remains active.

The native controls are the same-day edge201 GPU runs. These are single timed batches, with whole before stream and native controls run earlier. PD adds an RNGD, so these are not equal-resource efficiency or stable-speedup claims. The Qwen0.6B long-input point favors streaming PD, while its128-input point favors native (184.53 vs91.54 tok/s at output128/c4). The other long-input throughput points do not exceed native. Median E2E and aggregate throughput can rank differently; all per-condition first-SSE/E2E/estimated-TPOT metrics are retained in the comparison JSON.

Qwen4B matches native for both128-input references, but both8K references first differ at index12 before EOS. Gemma reaches EOS at index2 for input128 and has no EOS within the measured8K outputs. Fixed-length equality does not establish general answer quality or negate earlier numerical/quality counterexamples. Historical same-route c16 references also match4/4 for every arm.

## Reproduction

Use the same frozen checkpoints and research source wrapper as the preceding pdmid201 run: compiled width8192, fusion4/Mesh2, BF16 decoder, four decode slots, context9216, paged KV/page64, local chunk256, both caches off. This is the research RNGD engine, not vendor native furiosa-llm.

```bash
PYTHONPATH="$RUNTIME/opt_prime:$RUNTIME/opt_prime/examples/pd:$RNGD_SOURCE:$SERVING_MATRIX" OMP_NUM_THREADS=4 MKL_NUM_THREADS=4 TORCH_DEVICE_BACKEND_AUTOLOAD=0 HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 TRANSFORMERS_OFFLINE=1 "$PYTHON" "$HARNESS" --model "$MODEL" --mode pd --server-config "$CONFIG" --lengths 128 8192 --outputs 32 128 --concurrency 1 4 --repeats 1 --startup-timeout 1800 --request-timeout 180 --output "$NEW_RESULT"
# Stream config adds --pd-prefill-stream-layers; add --reference "$WHOLE_RESULT" to the harness.
python3 evidence265/audit_pd_gpu_edge201.py "$ARTIFACT_DIR"
python3 evidence265/selfcheck_pd_gpu_edge201.py "$ARTIFACT_DIR"
```

Use benchmark201_native_http.py and an idle allocated GPU/RNGD. The coordinator stops rather than modifying another workload if the GPU process query is nonempty or the selected RNGD has holders. Exact argv, config/source hashes, versions, exits and device identity are retained in pdedge201-gpu-queue.json. Raw names are pdedge201-{model}-{whole,stream}.json; the auditor also requires edge201 native and matching historical stream265 c16 controls. Site-local paths/configs/logs are not published. Summary and raw hashes are in `../evidence265/pdedge201-gpu-summary.json`; native comparison and EOS positions are in `../evidence265/pdedge201-gpu-comparison.json`.

## Verification and limits

Ten selfchecks cover a valid partial subset, request/aggregate output-length separation, duplicate complete arms, corrupt warmup/token/SSE/throughput/resource fields, missing conditions and missing raw data. No raw measurements or production engine behavior changed.

This completes these cache-off short/long HTTP controls only. Native equivalence, answer quality, cache-on behavior, supported exports and reliable performance under repeated load remain separate. CPU/client timestamps do not prove device DMA overlap. The full four-model goal is not complete.
