## Conclusion
Execution shape explains the early32-token discrepancy but does not establish full numerical equivalence. Residual long-output drift needs matched-prefix logit/KV diagnosis, tracked in#256. The retained-cache control argues against a unique live handoff corruption as the explanation for the32-token difference, but does not certify every transfer or arithmetic operator. Neither parity nor these capital-question fixtures certify model quality. Separate ATOM-prefetch performance validation is not evidence this GPU numerical boundary is fixed.
