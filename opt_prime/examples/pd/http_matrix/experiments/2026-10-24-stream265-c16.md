## Question

Does the same-source whole/stream behavior remain exact and beneficial when16requests queue behind4 execution slots?

## Setup

One source process/model, sourcewidth8192,4source/decoder slots, context8320/page64, cachesoff. Sixteen prompts alternate2048/8192tokens eight times; fixed128output without EOS stopping. Whole/stream warmups, then measured mode order whole/stream/stream/whole/whole/stream. Every request output compares to its corresponding first whole reference. This tests queue pressure with4execution slots, not a16-lane graph. Same local device allocation as the earlier c4 experiment; no competing owned local workload at launch.

## Command

Executed driver and queue are archived under evidence265. The queue records its exact environment and model/checkpoint paths.

```bash
python3 /tmp/stream265-c16-queue.py > /tmp/stream265-c16-queue.log 2>&1
python3 evidence265/audit_stream265_c16.py evidence265
python3 evidence265/selfcheck_stream265_c16_audit.py
```

Place the archived queue/driver at their recorded paths and use the source registration wrapper from the main streaming experiment. The queue launches each model sequentially with the recorded pt213 interpreter. Auditor verifies all16x128-token arrays, exact per-request parity, arrivals, mode/warmup order, progress counts,4-slot metadata and allfour child exits. It exits2 while any model is incomplete. Its synthetic self-check rejects corrupted tokens, shortened TTFT arrays, changed slots, failed child exits and missing models; these synthetic cases are verifier checks, not hardware measurements.

## Conclusion

Allfour models retain same-route parity under this queued workload. Qwen/KONI completion reductions are20–25%; Gemma is2.33%. These three-sample synthetic fixed-output results do not establish native numerical equivalence, semantic answer quality, HTTP behavior, device DMA overlap, or universal speedup. The earlier deferred-notification control shows total gains cannot be attributed entirely to within-request overlap. Whole transfer remains default. GPU/ATOM HTTPc16 work is tracked separately and is not part of these results.
